Why sponsor 612 Promise?

612 Promise fields a full-contact tackle team every fall in The League (AYFL Metro), and runs flag football and regional 7v7 tournaments year-round — serving kids in 3rd–8th grade across South Minneapolis, including families near Armitage, Lynnhurst, Pearl Park, and Linden Hills Park.

Sponsorship dollars go directly to gear, transportation (we run two 12-passenger vans), league fees, and coaching — and because we're a 501(c)(3), your contribution is tax-deductible.
